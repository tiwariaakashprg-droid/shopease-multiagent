CANCELLATION POLICY

Orders may be cancelled only before they are shipped.

Customers may withdraw their purchase request before dispatch.

A customer may revoke an order if payment has been completed but shipping has not started.

Purchase requests that have already been dispatched cannot be rescinded.

Customers who no longer need an item may request order cancellation before shipment.

An order that has entered the packing stage may not be eligible for cancellation.

Cancellation requests must include the order ID and registered email address.

Cancelled orders are refunded within 2-3 business days.

Customers may stop order processing by contacting support immediately.

A request to terminate an order after dispatch will be redirected to the return process.

Orders placed by mistake can be withdrawn before shipment.

Customers may change their decision and cancel the purchase before dispatch.

Cancellation is not possible after the package has left the warehouse.

A cancellation request submitted after shipping will be rejected automatically.

Orders that cannot be cancelled may still be returned after delivery according to the return policy.
