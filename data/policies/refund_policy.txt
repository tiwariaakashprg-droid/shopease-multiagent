REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of delivery.

Refund requests for orders below Rs 5000 are processed within 3-5 business days.

Refund requests above Rs 5000 require manager approval and may take 5-7 business days.

Customers may receive reimbursement if they accidentally ordered the wrong item.

Payments may be reversed if the customer cancels the order before shipping.

Money-back requests are accepted only for eligible products.

Reimbursement is not provided for intentionally damaged products.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Customers may recover their payment after successful return verification.

Compensation may be provided in exceptional situations approved by support.

The amount paid by the customer may be returned after inspection.

Refund processing time may vary during holidays and high-demand periods.

Customers can reclaim the amount they paid by submitting their order ID.

A payment reversal request must include proof of purchase.

Refund requests submitted after the allowed period may be rejected.
