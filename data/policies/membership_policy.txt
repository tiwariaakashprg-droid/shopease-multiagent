INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING POLICY

ShopEase provides delivery services to selected international destinations.

Delivery times for international orders generally range from 7 to 21 business days.

Customs inspections may increase the shipping duration.

Customers are responsible for import duties, taxes, and customs charges imposed by their country.

International shipments may have limited tracking availability depending on the destination.

Orders shipped outside India may experience delays because of customs clearance procedures.

The estimated arrival date for overseas deliveries is approximate and cannot be guaranteed.

Some products cannot be delivered internationally because of local regulations.

Customers should verify whether a product is eligible for international delivery before placing an order.

International parcels may pass through multiple logistics partners before reaching the final destination.

Lost international packages are investigated with the shipping carrier before compensation is considered.

Express international delivery options may be available for selected countries.

Shipping charges for overseas orders depend on package weight and destination.

Customers may contact support regarding customs delays or import restrictions.

Refunds for international shipping fees are not provided unless the shipment fails because of ShopEase errors.
