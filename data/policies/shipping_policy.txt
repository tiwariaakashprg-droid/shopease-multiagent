SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY

Standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days.

Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and requires an additional charge.

Orders above Rs 500 qualify for free standard shipping.

Customers can monitor shipment status using the tracking ID sent by email.

Packages delayed by more than 5 days automatically qualify for a Rs 50 coupon.

International deliveries may require additional customs processing time.

Customers can track their parcel through the ShopEase tracking portal.

A shipment that has not reached the customer within the expected delivery window may be considered delayed.

Late deliveries caused by weather conditions, strikes, or natural disasters are not eligible for compensation.

Delivery estimates are approximate and may vary depending on the customer's location.

Parcels that are in transit can still be tracked using the shipment number.

Customers may inquire about the expected arrival date of their package.

Orders shipped to remote regions may require extra delivery time.

International shipping charges depend on the destination country and package weight.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available after dispatch.
