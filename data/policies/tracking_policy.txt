ORDER TRACKING POLICY

Every shipped order receives a unique tracking ID.

Customers can monitor the movement of their parcel using the tracking number.

Tracking information becomes available within 24 hours of dispatch.

A shipment may remain in transit for several days depending on the destination.

Customers can check the current status of their package through the ShopEase tracking portal.

If tracking information is unavailable, customers should wait at least 24 hours after shipping.

Packages moving between logistics hubs may not show frequent updates.

Customers may contact support if the parcel location has not changed for more than 72 hours.

Tracking status such as 'In Transit', 'Out for Delivery', and 'Delivered' are automatically updated.

Orders that are delayed due to weather conditions or transportation issues may experience slower tracking updates.

The expected arrival date is an estimate and may change.

Tracking IDs are sent through email and SMS after dispatch.

Customers can inquire about shipment progress using their order number.

International shipments may have limited tracking information in certain countries.

A parcel that has not reached the destination may still be in transit.
