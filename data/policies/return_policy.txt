RETURN POLICY

Customers may return products within 7 days of delivery.

Returned items must be unused and remain in their original packaging.

Customers may send an item back if they are not satisfied with the purchase.

A product can be given back only after obtaining a return authorization.

Items that have been used, damaged by the customer, or modified are not eligible for return.

Customers who no longer want an item may initiate a return request.

Products purchased by mistake may be returned according to the eligibility conditions.

A customer may exchange an eligible item for another product.

The return process requires the order ID and proof of purchase.

Customers may request pickup of the item that needs to be sent back.

Products returned without accessories may not qualify for a full refund.

Items returned after the allowed period may be rejected.

The company reserves the right to inspect all returned products before approval.

Customers may give back defective products if they are not eligible for replacement.

The process of sending back an item generally takes 3-5 business days.
