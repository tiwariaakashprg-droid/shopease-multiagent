DAMAGED AND DEFECTIVE PRODUCT POLICY

Customers who receive a damaged product must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.

Products with manufacturing defects are eligible for free replacement or full reimbursement.

Broken items, cracked screens, missing accessories, or physically damaged components qualify for replacement.

Customers who receive faulty or unusable products may request compensation.

Products that stop functioning shortly after delivery may be considered defective.

A non-working charger, dead display, or malfunctioning device is treated as a defective product.

Items arriving with missing parts are eligible for replacement after verification.

Customers must upload photographs of the damaged item as proof.

Replacement shipping costs for defective products are covered by ShopEase.

Reimbursement may be issued if replacement inventory is unavailable.

Products that arrive in poor condition may qualify for a refund.

Manufacturing faults and hardware failures are covered under this policy.

Shattered screens, broken hinges, and damaged packaging are considered valid complaints.

Defective products must be inspected before approval of replacement or refund.

Customers who receive an unusable device should immediately contact support.
